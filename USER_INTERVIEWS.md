

# User Interviews - AI Spend Audit

## Interview 1 - May 13, 2026

**Name:** R. Kumar (friend)
**Role:** Full-stack Developer
**Company stage:** Freelance, solo developer
**Interview method:** Voice call (15 minutes)

**3+ direct quotes:**

1. "I pay for Cursor Pro ($20) and ChatGPT Plus ($20). That's $40/month but I don't really track it."
2. "I never thought about whether I'm overpaying. I just pay the bill and move on."
3. "I would definitely use a free audit tool, but if you ask for my email before showing results, I'm closing the tab."

**The most surprising thing they said:**
"He didn't know Cursor has a free tier. He assumed only paid plans exist."

**What it changed about my design:**
"I made sure the form shows all plan options including free tiers. I also put the email gate AFTER the results."

## Interview 2 - May 13, 2026

**Name:** S. Sharma (colleague)
**Role:** Product Manager at a startup
**Company stage:** Seed stage, 12 employees
**Interview method:** Video call (12 minutes)

**3+ direct quotes:**

1. "We use Cursor, Copilot, and ChatGPT across the team. Probably spending $500+/month."
2. "I've wondered if we could save money by consolidating, but I don't have time to research pricing."
3. "The benchmark feature is really interesting - I want to know if we're spending more than other startups our size."

**The most surprising thing they said:**
"She tracks her AWS spending weekly but has never once looked at her AI tool spending. It's an afterthought."

**What it changed about my design:**
"I added the benchmark mode to compare against industry averages. That was her biggest question."

---

## Interview 3 - May 13, 2026

**Name:** T. Patel (university senior)
**Role:** CS Student / Freelancer
**Company stage:** Student with side projects
**Interview method:** Discord call (10 minutes)

**3+ direct quotes:**

1. "I use Copilot free as a student. That's the only AI tool I pay for."
2. "I wouldn't trust a tool that recommends random tools I've never heard of."
3. "If the savings seem too good to be true, I'd assume it's a scam."

**The most surprising thing they said:**
"He's a student but still thinks about AI tool costs. His trust threshold is very low - any suspicious recommendation would lose him."

**What it changed about my design:**
"I made sure every recommendation includes a specific reason with pricing logic. No vague 'you can save money' statements."

---

## Key Takeaways for Final Product

| Insight | Action Taken |
| :--- | :--- |
| Users don't track AI spending | Make the tool dead simple |
| Email gate must be after value | Email form appears AFTER results |
| Free tiers are unknown | Show all plan options clearly |
| Benchmark is valuable | Added benchmark mode |
| Trust is critical | Show reasoning for every recommendation |
| Students/freelancers care too | Keep tool free forever |
